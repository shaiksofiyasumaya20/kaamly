# 🌐 KAAMLY — Community Service Bidding Platform

**Find trusted local workers. Compare bids. Choose the right service at the right price.**

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://shaiksofiyasumaya20.github.io/kaamly/

## 📌 About the Project

Kaamly is a community-focused service bidding platform designed to connect people who need household services with local workers who can provide them.

Instead of contacting workers individually, users can post a service request and compare competing price offers from available workers before making a choice.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Finding reliable local service providers can be difficult. Customers may struggle to compare prices, discover available workers, and make informed hiring decisions.

At the same time, local workers need a convenient way to discover potential customers and compete for service opportunities.

## 💡 My Solution

Kaamly aims to bring customers and local service providers together through a simple, transparent bidding experience.

- Customers post household service requirements.
- Workers review requests and submit price bids.
- Customers compare available offers.
- Customers choose the worker that best meets their needs.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📝 **Post Service Requests** — Describe the household service you need.
- 💰 **Competitive Bidding** — Allow workers to offer different prices.
- ⚖️ **Compare Offers** — Evaluate available bids before choosing.
- 🏘️ **Community-Focused** — Connect customers with local service providers.
- 🎨 **Modern Interface** — Designed for a clear and accessible user experience.

*Note: Features depend on the current implementation of the application.*
